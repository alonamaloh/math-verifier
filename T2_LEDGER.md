## Wave order and parallelism

Roughly a third of Part II is plane-free and can start the day Part I starts: T2.1's graph-relabelling module, T2.2's abstract `library/Cellulation/` block (cell structures, both elementary operations, the generated-structure induction, boundary cycles), T3.1b's cyclic-order package, and T2.4a's `Refines`/star/limit-map material — none of these mention `inside`, `Boundary`, a Jordan curve or a crosscut, and the first two build in the ~14 s `make -j 16 graph` inner loop rather than the plane cone.
